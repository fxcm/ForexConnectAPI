public method IReceiveBarQuotesCallback::nextBarQuote

Brief

The method is called for every bar received.

Declaration
C++
virtual void  nextBarQuote (IReceiveBarQuotesTask *task, double date, double bidOpen, double bidHigh, double bidLow, double bidClose, double bidVolume, double askOpen, double askHigh, double askLow, double askClose, double askVolume) = 0

Parameters
task

The task being executed.
date

The date/time when the bar starts.
bidOpen

The open price of the bid bar.
bidHigh

The high price of the bid bar.
bidLow

The low price of the bid bar.
bidClose

The close price of the bid bar.
bidVolume

The tick volume.

Note: this value is equal to askVolume.
askOpen

The open price of the ask bar.
askHigh

The high price of the ask bar.
askLow

The low price of the ask bar.
askClose

The close price of the ask bar.
askVolume

The tick volume.

Note: this value is equal to bidVolume.

Details

The method is called for older bars in the requested time first, so the data received will be "sorted" by the time in the ascending order.

For more information about the meaning of the values, read What is Price?.
