trade_distribution Table

Brief

The article describes the Trade Distribution table of the report.

Details

The Trade Distribution table contains information about allocation of funds realized from all positions that were closed during the report statement period. The funds are allocated (distributed) among all managed accounts mapped to the funds manager account. Each row of the table contains information about one allocation made from one closed position to one managed account. So, allocations from one closed position are recorded in several rows depending on the number of managed accounts. The identifier of the table is trade_distribution.

All data rows are arranged in ascending order by the unique identification number (ticket_id) of the closed position.

If no information is found for the report statement period, the table has only a header row.

Column identifier

Column data type

Description

ticket_id

INTEGER

The unique identification number of the position.

manager_account

STRING

The identifier of the funds manager account (as it is displayed in the Trading Station).

manager_gross_pl

DOUBLE

The amount of profit or loss realized during the entire lifetime of the position, i.e. from its open date to the close date. This amount excludes additional charges, such as broker commission (manager_comm), rollover interest (manager_rollover), and adjustment (manager_adj).

The value can be either positive or negative. A positive value indicates profit, a negative value indicates loss.

The manager gross profit/loss is expressed in the account denomination currency.

manager_comm

DOUBLE

The amount of broker commission charged during the entire lifetime of the position, i.e. from its open date to the close date.

The value can be either negative or zero. A negative value indicates that the commission was charged, a zero value indicates that the commission was not charged.

The manager commission is expressed in the account denomination currency.

manager_dividends

DOUBLE

Not used.

manager_rollover

DOUBLE

The amount of rollover interest earned or paid during the entire lifetime of the position, i.e. from its open date to the close date.

The value can be either positive or negative. A positive value indicates that the rollover was earned, a negative value indicates that the rollover was paid.

The manager rollover is expressed in the account denomination currency.

manager_adj

DOUBLE

The amount of adjustment applied to the gross profit/loss (manager_gross_pl) for the period from the close date of the position to the current date and time.

The value can be either positive or negative. A positive value indicates that the gross profit/loss encreased, a negative value indicates that the gross profit/loss decreased.

The manager adjustment is expressed in the account denomination currency.

manager_net_pl

DOUBLE

The amount of profit or loss realized during the entire lifetime of the position, i.e. from its open date to the close date. This amount includes all charges, such as broker commission (manager_comm), rollover interest (manager_rollover), and adjustment (manager_adj).

The value can be either positive or negative. A positive value indicates profit, a negative value indicates loss.

The manager net profit/loss is expressed in the account denomination currency.

controlled_account

STRING

The identifier of the managed account (as it is displayed in the Trading Station).

controlled_percent

DOUBLE

The allocation percentage that represents how much of the profit, loss, and fees must be allocated to the managed account.

The allocation percentage is calculated at the end of each trading day based on the ratio of the managed account balance to the aggregate balance of the funds manager account.

controlled_gross_pl

DOUBLE

The amount of gross profit or loss allocated to the managed account. This amount is obtained from the manager gross profit/loss (manager_gross_pl) based on the allocation percentage (controlled_percent) of the managed account. This amount excludes additional charges, such as broker commission (controlled_comm), rollover interest (controlled_rollover), and adjustment (controlled_adj).

The value can be either positive or negative. A positive value indicates profit, a negative value indicates loss.

The controlled gross profit/loss is expressed in the account denomination currency.

controlled_comm

DOUBLE

The amount of broker commission charged on the managed account. This amount is obtained from the manager commission (manager_comm) based on the allocation percentage (controlled_percent) of the managed account.

The value can be either negative or zero. A negative value indicates that the commission was charged, a zero value indicates that the commission was not charged.

The controlled commission is expressed in the account denomination currency.

controlled_dividends

DOUBLE

Not used.

controlled_rollover

DOUBLE

The amount of rollover interest earned or paid by the managed account. This amount is obtained from the manager rollover (manager_rollover) based on the allocation percentage (controlled_percent) of the managed account.

The value can be either positive or negative. A positive value indicates that the rollover was earned, a negative value indicates that the rollover was paid.

The controlled rollover is expressed in the account denomination currency.

controlled_adj

DOUBLE

The amount of adjustment applied to the gross profit/loss (controlled_gross_pl) of the managed account. This amount is obtained from the manager adjustment (manager_adj) based on the allocation percentage (controlled_percent) of the managed account.

The value can be either positive or negative. A positive value indicates that the gross profit/loss encreased, a negative value indicates that the gross profit/loss decreased.

The controlled adjustment is expressed in the account denomination currency.

controlled_net_pl

DOUBLE

The amount of profit or loss allocated to the managed account. This amount represents the sum of the controlled gross profit/loss (controlled_gross_pl), broker commission (controlled_comm), rollover interest (controlled_rollover), and adjustment (controlled_adj).

The value can be either positive or negative. A positive value indicates profit, a negative value indicates loss.

The controlled net profit/loss is expressed in the account denomination currency.
