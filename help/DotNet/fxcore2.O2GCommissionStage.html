enum O2GCommissionStage

Brief

The enum specifies a set of constants representing stages when commission is incurred.

Details

The stage can be obtained using the O2GCommissionDescription.Stage property.

Static Public Fields

AnyDealCommission

Commission is divided evenly between on the opening or closing of the position.

CloseCommission

Entire commission is incurred on the closing of the position.

CommissionStageUnknown

The constant represents an unknown commission stage.

OpenCommission

Entire commission is incurred on the opening of the position.
