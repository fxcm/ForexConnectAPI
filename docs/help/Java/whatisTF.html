What is Timeframe?

Brief

The article describes what the timeframe is.

Details

What is Timeframe?

A candle (or bar) represents open (the first), high (the maximum), low (the minimum) and close (the last) prices reached during a certain period of time. This kind of price aggregation is used for easier presentation of the price history and for technical analysis.

The period length is typically a particular number of minutes (e.g. 1 minute, 5 minutes), hours (e.g. 1 hour, 2 hours), days, weeks or months. This period of time is called the timeframe.

The timeframe defines not only the length of the time period, but also the moment when the time period starts.

1 minute periods always start at 00 seconds of that minute.

The timeframes of a number of minutes are always aligned against an hour, e.g. 15-minute periods always start at HH:00:00, HH:15:00, HH:30:00, and HH:45:00.

1 hour periods always start at 00 minutes 00 seconds of that hour.

1 day period starts when the market is closed and re-opened. For the Forex market it is usually 17:00 New York time. So, 16:59:59 is the last second of today's day candle and 17:00:00 is the first second of tomorrow's day candle. In order to calculate the period borders properly, we should know an offset of the trading day start. This is the number of hours before (negative value) or after (positive value) midnight of the calendar date. So, if the trading day starts at 17:00 of yesterday, the trading day start offset is -7 hours. If the trading day starts at 1:00 of today, the trading day offset is +1 hour.

Please note that for the Forex market the time is in New York time. For UTC, the trading day start will be either 21:00 or 22:00 depending on whether New York is in daylight saving time or not. The trading week is the 7-day period started at the trading day of Sunday (17:00NYT of Saturday). The trading month is the month period started at the start of the first trading day of the month (e.g. June starts at 17:00NYT, May 31 and ends at 17:00NYT, Jun 30).

The timeframes of a number of hours are aligned against the start of the trading day. So, 6-hour periods will start at 17:00NYT, 23:00NYT, 5:00NYT, and 11:00NYT.

Timeframe Names

The name of the timeframe is the name of the timeframe measurement unit followed by the length of the time period.

The names of the units are:

Name

Meaning

Example(s)

t

Ticks

t1 - ticks.

m

Minutes

m1 - 1 minute, m5 - 5 minutes, m30 - 30 minutes.

H

Hours

H1 - 1 hour, H6 - 6 hours, H12 - 12 hours.

D

Days

D1 - 1 day.

W

Weeks

W1 - 1 week.

M

Months

M1 - 1 month.
